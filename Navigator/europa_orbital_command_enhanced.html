POWER STRATEGY

Europa Clipper is powered by large solar arrays, not an RTG. Its arrays are designed to supply at least 700 W throughout the prime mission at Jupiter; output is much higher nearer the Sun. This station models a fictional Karadi power bus, with adjustable subsystem priorities.

RTGs are different: they convert heat from radioactive decay into electricity. They are not used by Europa Clipper.
